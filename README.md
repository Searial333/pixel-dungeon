# Pixel Dungeon - EverQuest II Edition

A complete conversion of Pixel Dungeon into an authentic **EverQuest II roguelike experience**, featuring the beloved MMORPG's races, classes, lore, and world.

## 🌟 Features

### **19 Playable Races**
From the honorable Frogloks of Qeynos to the dark Tier'Dal of Freeport, plus the neutral vampire Freebloods. Each race provides unique stat bonuses that stack with deity blessings.

### **24 Fully Developed Subclasses**
Every subclass has:
- ✅ **2-3 Unique Active Abilities**
- ✅ **Passive Trait Bonuses** (+damage, +defense, +crit, etc.)
- ✅ **Distinct Playstyle** (Tank, DPS, Healer, Support)

**Examples:**
- **Guardian**: -20% damage taken, Shield Bash + Provoke
- **Wizard**: +30% spell damage, Fireball + Ice Comet + Arcane Barrage
- **Assassin**: +50% backstab, +15% crit, Backstab + Eviscerate

### **The Four Pillars Stat System**
- **Durability**: HP, armor, stamina (Fighters)
- **Mysticism**: Mana, spell damage, healing (Mages, Priests)
- **Skill**: Accuracy, evasion, criticals (Scouts)
- **Presence**: Healing power, charm, buffs (Priests, Bards)

### **12 Deities to Worship**
Good, Neutral, and Evil gods providing powerful stat bonuses:
- **Mithaniel Marr** (Light): +2 Dur, +1 Skill, +2 Presence
- **Solusek Ro** (Fire): +4 Mysticism
- **Innoruuk** (Hate): +2 Dur, +2 Myst, +1 Skill

### **40+ Themed Items**
- **20 Epic Weapons**: Fiery Avenger, Innoruuk's Curse, Frostreaver, etc.
- **10 Legendary Armor Sets**: Class-specific with rich lore
- **10 Deity Rings**: Divine powers (cold immunity, lifetap boost, etc.)
- **Consumables**: Mana potions, Elven Waybread, Dwarven Ale, Halfling Pipeweed

### **3 Legendary Raid Bosses**
- **Lady Vox** (500 HP): Ice dragon with freezing attacks
- **Lord Trakanon** (600 HP): Undead dragon with deadly poison
- **Venril Sathir** (450 HP): Iksar lich who summons minions

### **Heritage Quest System**
Multi-step epic quest chains rewarding legendary weapons:
- The Fiery Avenger (Paladin)
- Innoruuk's Curse (Shadowknight)
- Staff of Eternal Flame (Wizard)

### **Alternate Advancement (AA)**
Endgame progression with 5 powerful AA abilities:
- **Perfect Form**: Ultimate stat buff
- **Time Warp**: Act twice
- **Divine Intervention**: Prevent death
- **Second Wind**: Emergency heal
- **Mana Regeneration**: Passive ranks

## 🎮 Quick Start

1. **Choose Race** → Affects base stats
2. **Choose Archetype** → Defines resources (HP/Mana/Stamina)
3. **Level 3: Choose Subclass** → Unlocks signature abilities
4. **Choose Deity** → Additional stat bonuses
5. **Explore Norrath!** → Antonica → Blackburrow → Crushbone → Lavastorm
