# Complete Class Abilities Reference - EverQuest II Edition

## Fighter Subclasses

### Guardian - The Unbreakable Wall
**Passive**: -20% damage taken, improved threat generation
**Abilities**:
- **Provoke** (30 stam, 10s CD) - Taunt all nearby enemies to attack you
- **Shield Bash** (35 stam, 12s CD) - Stun enemy with your shield

### Berserker - Fury Incarnate
**Passive**: +25-50% damage (scales with missing HP), +10% damage taken
**Abilities**:
- **Berserk Rage** (40 stam) - Increase damage, decrease defense
- **Rampage** (45 stam, 15s CD) - AoE whirlwind attack hitting all adjacent enemies

### Monk - Martial Arts Master
**Passive**: +15% evasion, +15% damage when lightly armored, +2 stamina regen
**Abilities**:
- **Flying Kick** (40 stam, 10s CD) - Leap kick that damages and stuns

### Bruiser - Crowd Control Brawler
**Passive**: +20% damage, increased stun duration
**Abilities**:
- **Haymaker** (45 stam, 12s CD) - Devastating punch that dazes the enemy

### Paladin - Holy Warrior
**Passive**: +10% damage vs undead, -10% damage taken, lay on hands
**Abilities**:
- **Lay on Hands** (40 mana, 120s CD) - Emergency self-heal for 50% HP
- **Shield Bash** (35 stam, 12s CD) - Stun enemy with your shield

### Shadowknight - Dark Knight
**Passive**: +15% damage, 5% lifesteal, -10% damage taken
**Abilities**:
- **Lifetap** (25 mana, 8s CD) - Drain enemy life to heal yourself
- **Harm Touch** (50 mana, 600s CD) - Devastating dark damage touch attack

## Priest Subclasses

### Cleric - Master Healer
**Passive**: +20% healing power, group heal efficiency
**Abilities**:
- **Celestial Healing** (50 mana) - Powerful single target heal
- **Group Heal** (60 mana, 15s CD) - Heal all nearby allies
- **Spiritual Renewal** (40 mana) - Healing over time

### Druid - Nature's Champion
**Passive**: +15% nature spell damage, +10% healing over time
**Abilities**:
- **Thorncoat** (35 mana, 20s CD) - Cover yourself in damage-reflecting thorns
- **Spiritual Renewal** (40 mana) - Healing over time
- **Regrowth** (25 mana, 10s CD) - Powerful healing over time

### Shaman - Spirit Caller
**Passive**: +15% buff/debuff duration, totem power
**Abilities**:
- **Spirit Totem** (50 mana, 30s CD) - Place a healing totem
- **Spiritual Renewal** (40 mana) - Healing over time

### Templar - Armored Healer
**Passive**: -10% damage taken, +15% reactive healing
**Abilities**:
- **Reactive Heal** (40 mana, 25s CD) - Ward that auto-heals when damaged
- **Group Heal** (60 mana, 15s CD) - Heal all nearby allies

### Inquisitor - Offensive Healer
**Passive**: +25% damage vs undead, +10% holy damage
**Abilities**:
- **Smite Evil** (30 mana, 8s CD) - Holy damage, extra vs undead
- **Celestial Healing** (50 mana) - Powerful single target heal

### Warden - HoT Specialist
**Passive**: +25% healing over time potency
**Abilities**:
- **Regrowth** (25 mana, 10s CD) - Powerful healing over time
- **Thorncoat** (35 mana, 20s CD) - Damage-reflecting thorns

### Fury - Offensive Druid
**Passive**: +20% elemental damage, +10% healing
**Abilities**:
- **Tempest** (70 mana, 20s CD) - Storm that damages all nearby enemies
- **Spiritual Renewal** (40 mana) - Healing over time

### Defiler - Plague Shaman
**Passive**: +20% DoT damage, +15% debuff potency
**Abilities**:
- **Plague** (60 mana, 25s CD) - Spread disease to all nearby enemies
- **Spirit Totem** (50 mana, 30s CD) - Place a totem

### Mystic - Ward Specialist
**Passive**: +20% ward strength, +10% vision range
**Abilities**:
- **Mystic Ward** (45 mana, 20s CD) - Protective barrier that absorbs damage
- **Spiritual Renewal** (40 mana) - Healing over time

## Mage Subclasses

### Wizard - Elemental Destruction
**Passive**: +30% elemental damage, +1 mana regen
**Abilities**:
- **Fireball** (50 mana, 10s CD) - Area of effect fire damage
- **Ice Comet** (60 mana) - Cold damage + slow
- **Arcane Barrage** (45 mana, 8s CD) - Rapid fire magic missiles

### Warlock - Chaos Magic
**Passive**: Chaos effects, +50% damage variance, +1 mana regen
**Abilities**:
- **Chaos Blast** (55 mana, 12s CD) - Unpredictable chaotic energy with random effects
- **Fireball** (50 mana, 10s CD) - Area of effect fire damage

### Sorcerer - Versatile Caster
**Passive**: +15% spell damage, +2 mana regen
**Abilities**:
- **Arcane Barrage** (45 mana, 8s CD) - Rapid fire magic missiles
- **Fireball** (50 mana, 10s CD) - Area of effect fire damage

### Summoner - Pet Master
**Passive**: +50% pet damage/HP, +1 max summon
**Abilities**:
- **Summon Elemental** (80 mana, 60s CD) - Call forth an elemental to fight
- **Ice Comet** (60 mana) - Cold damage + slow

### Necromancer - Death Magic
**Passive**: +15% DoT damage, undead control, +1 mana regen
**Abilities**:
- **Lifetap** (25 mana, 8s CD) - Drain enemy life to heal yourself
- **Raise Dead** (60 mana, 45s CD) - Animate a skeleton warrior

### Illusionist - Crowd Control
**Passive**: +50% crowd control duration
**Abilities**:
- **Mesmerize** (35 mana, 12s CD) - Put enemy to sleep
- **Mind Control** (70 mana, 30s CD) - Dominate enemy's mind

### Coercer - Mind Control
**Passive**: +50% charm duration, mana drain on hit
**Abilities**:
- **Mesmerize** (35 mana, 12s CD) - Put enemy to sleep

## Ability Cost Legend
- **Stamina**: Physical resource for fighters and scouts
- **Mana**: Magical resource for priests and mages
- **CD**: Cooldown time in seconds

## Synergy Tips
1. **Tank Synergy**: Guardian + Cleric = Unbreakable defense
2. **DPS Synergy**: Wizard + Troubadour = Massive spell damage
3. **Control Synergy**: Illusionist + Dirge = Crowd control lockdown
4. **Hybrid Synergy**: Shadowknight + Defiler = Dark damage overtime
5. **Pet Synergy**: Summoner + Ranger = Army of companions
