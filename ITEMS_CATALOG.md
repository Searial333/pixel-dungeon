## 🗡️ Legendary Weapons

### Tier 1 - Signature Weapons
- **Fiery Avenger** (Sword) - Paladin blade with fire damage (25% burn chance)
- **Innoruuk's Curse** (Sword) - Shadowknight blade with lifetap
- **Water Sprinkler of Nem Ankh** (Mace) - Cleric's holy mace
- **Earthcaller** (Staff) - Shaman staff that roots enemies

- **Raging Blades** (Dual Swords) - Berserker paired weapons

- **Blade of Chaos** (Sword) - Warlock chaos damage with random effects
- **Nature's Wrath** (Staff) - Druid/Warden staff (25% root chance)
- **Ghoulbane** (Mace) - Cleric/Templar mace (bonus holy damage vs undead)
- **Twin Fangs of the Serpent** (Daggers) - Swashbuckler dual daggers (poison + double strike)
- **Scythe of Sorrow** (Scythe) - Necromancer weapon (lifetap effect)
- **Mind Blade** (Dagger) - Illusionist/Coercer weapon (confuses enemies)
- **Crimson Tempest** (Dagger) - Assassin blade (bleeding effect)
- **Lute of Enchantment** (Instrument) - Troubadour/Dirge weapon (charm + mana regen)

### Tier 3 - Elemental Weapons ⭐ NEW!

- **Soulreaper's Scythe** (Scythe) - Death weapon (40% lifetap)

## 🎯 Item Synergies

### Tank Build (Guardian/Paladin)
- **Armor**: Plate of Eternal Vigilance
- **Weapon**: Fiery Avenger or Ghoulbane
- **Ring**: Ring of Tunare's Blessing
- **Consumable**: Dwarven Ale (durability boost)

### DPS Caster Build (Wizard/Warlock)
- **Armor**: Robes of the Archmage
- **Weapon**: Staff of Eternal Flame or Blade of Chaos
- **Ring**: Ring of the Erudite Scholar
- **Consumable**: Potion of Mana Restoration

### Stealth Build (Assassin/Brigand)
- **Armor**: Leathers of the Silent Hunter
- **Weapon**: Crimson Tempest or Twin Fangs
- **Ring**: Ring of Bristlebane's Luck
- **Consumable**: Halfling Pipeweed (skill boost)

### Healer Build (Cleric/Druid)
- **Armor**: Vestments of the High Priest
- **Weapon**: Water Sprinkler or Nature's Wrath
- **Ring**: Ring of Tunare's Blessing
- **Consumable**: Elven Waybread (healing)

### Dark Knight Build (Shadowknight/Necromancer)
- **Armor**: Shadowplate of Innoruuk
- **Weapon**: Innoruuk's Curse or Scythe of Sorrow
- **Ring**: Ring of the Shadow Knight
- **Consumable**: Potion of Cazic's Blessing
