# EverQuest II Edition - Features Guide

## 🎮 Welcome to Norrath!

This customized version of Pixel Dungeon has been transformed into a full-fledged **EverQuest II roguelike**, bringing the beloved MMORPG's lore, classes, races, and world to the dungeon-crawling experience.

---

## 🧙 Character Creation

### **19 Playable Races**

Choose from the iconic races of Norrath, each with unique stat bonuses:

#### **Good Aligned Races (Qeynos)**
- **Human** - Balanced (+1 to all stats)
- **Barbarian** - Fierce warriors (+3 Durability, +1 Skill)
- **High Elf** - Arcane masters (+3 Mysticism, +1 Presence)
- **Half Elf** - Versatile (+2 Mysticism, +1 Skill)
- **Dwarf** - Resilient defenders (+2 Durability, +1 Presence)
- **Gnome** - Clever tinkerers (+2 Mysticism, +2 Skill)
- **Halfling** - Lucky rogues (+3 Skill)
- **Erudite** - Brilliant scholars (+4 Mysticism, +1 Presence)
- **Kerra** - Feline hunters (+1 Durability, +2 Skill)
- **Fae** - Magical faeries (+3 Mysticism, +2 Presence)
- **Froglok** - Honorable warriors (+2 Durability, +1 Skill)

#### **Evil Aligned Races (Freeport)**
- **Dark Elf** - Shadow masters (+2 Mysticism, +1 Skill)
- **Iksar** - Scaled warriors (+3 Durability)
- **Troll** - Regenerating brutes (+4 Durability)
- **Ogre** - Devastating giants (+4 Durability)
- **Ratonga** - Cunning scavengers (+3 Skill)
- **Arasai** - Corrupted fae (+3 Mysticism, +2 Presence)
- **Sarnak** - Draconic fighters (+2 Durability, +1 Mysticism)

#### **Neutral Races (Can Choose Alignment)**
- **Freeblood** - Vampiric undead (−1 Durability, +2 Mysticism, +1 Skill, +3 Presence)
  * Vampires who broke free from the Bloodline curse
  * Can start in either Qeynos or Freeport
  * Supernatural presence and dark magical aptitude
  * Physically fragile but incredibly charismatic
  * Walk the path between light and shadow
  * **Perfect for:** Necromancers, Bards, Shadowknights, or any charisma-based build

### **4 Class Archetypes**

Begin your journey as one of four archetypes:

1. **Fighter** - Masters of melee combat and heavy armor
   - High Durability and Stamina
   - Starting HP: 22
   - Excels at tanking and physical damage

2. **Priest** - Divine healers and support specialists
   - High Mysticism and Presence
   - Large Mana pool (80)
   - Healing and buffing abilities

3. **Mage** - Arcane spellcasters wielding elemental magic
   - Exceptional Mysticism
   - Largest Mana pool (100)
   - Devastating spell damage

4. **Scout** - Agile combatants using stealth and precision
   - High Skill
   - Massive Stamina pool (150)
   - Critical strikes and evasion

## 📊 Stat System - The Four Pillars

### **D.A.D. (Durability)**
- Physical resilience, health, defense
- Important for: Fighters, tanks

### **M.A.D. (Mysticism)**
- Magical power, mana, spell effectiveness
- Important for: Mages, Priests

### **S.A.D. (Skill)**
- Accuracy, evasion, critical strikes, stealth
- Important for: Scouts, rogues

### **P.A.D. (Presence)**
- Charisma, awareness, companion abilities, healing power
- Important for: Priests, bards

## 🎮 Gameplay Tips

1. **Race + Deity Synergy**: Combine racial bonuses with deity blessings for massive stat boosts
2. **Class Abilities**: Use mana/stamina wisely - abilities have cooldowns
3. **Named Mobs**: Hunt for named enemies - they drop better loot!
4. **Quest Items**: Collect gnoll fangs, orc belts, and dragon scales
5. **Zone Progression**: Each zone has unique monsters and difficulty
6. **Equipment**: Legendary items are rare but incredibly powerful
